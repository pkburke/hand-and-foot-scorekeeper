HAND & FOOT SCOREKEEPER

A scorekeeping companion for Hand & Foot played with real cards. Supports
2 individual players, 4 players in two partnerships, or 6 players in three
partnerships. No account is needed. Works offline after the first online load.

Open the app: https://handandfoot.theburkeclann.com/
Test version: https://handandfoottest.theburkeclann.com/

GET STARTED

1. Open Games, choose + New game, name the game, and select the player count.
2. Enter player or team names on Scoreboard.
3. Use Reference for card values and requirements, or Rules for the searchable
   house rulebook. The app uses fixed house rules; check them before playing.
4. At round end, open Score. Calculated scoring walks through unplayed cards,
   completed books, and played cards. Include cards inside books in the played
   count as well as cards in incomplete melds, counting each card once.
5. Mark every player/team complete, select who went out, review the round,
   and confirm it. The +100 going-out bonus is added automatically.

Manual scoring is also available: enter played card points, penalties as a
positive number, and book bonuses separately. Leave the going-out bonus out
of these fields. Check that the side going out has the required books.

GAMES AND RESULTS

Scores and unfinished entries autosave. Keep multiple games, select Continue
to resume one, and find final results in Completed games. Play again creates
a fresh rematch with the same players and scoring method, keeping the original.
Remove moves a game to Removed games; Restore game brings it back.

Undo last entry works after leaving its field. Reloading, switching games,
importing, or reopening a saved round clears entry-undo history. Use Edit last
round on Scoreboard to correct the most recently saved round.

BACKUPS AND SETTINGS

Games are local to this browser, device, and site address. There is no automatic
sync. Use Export backup, confirm the JSON file is saved in Files or Downloads,
then Import backup to transfer games. Imports add to your existing collection.
Backups include removed games and unfinished entries. Use one app window for
scorekeeping. An older or secondary window pauses saving to protect newer
games; export its unfinished entries before reloading. If autosave fails, the
recovery notice offers Export backup and Try saving. Keep the app open and
export before reloading or leaving.

Settings offers System, Light, and Dark appearance. Install on your Home Screen
through your browser's sharing/install option. When an update is ready, choose
Update now or Later; a postponed update remains available in Settings.
